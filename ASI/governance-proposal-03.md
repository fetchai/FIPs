# Merging of OCEAN into ASI

The FET, AGIX and OCEAN team's plan to merge in order to lead the next generation of decentralized AI infrastructure.

This is the third of three governance proposals that we intend to bring before the community. It follows directly on from [Proposal 26](https://www.mintscan.io/fetchai/proposals/26)


## Tokenomics

Following on from the tokenomics section of the previous proposal, this proposal covers increasing the total supply of the ASI token to accommodate the OCEAN conversion to ASI.

Conversion from OCEAN to ASI will be one way and will be set at the following conversion rate:

* The OCEAN:ASI conversion ratio will be 1:0.433226. Therefore, if you currently hold ~2308 OCEAN this would convert to 1000 ASI.

Conversion will be done, where possible, automatically. However, holders of the OCEAN token might need to use conversion contracts and tools.


## Proposal Scope

**This proposal covers the merging of the OCEAN token into the ASI token**. The following is a summary of the scope of the proposal.

* The merging of the OCEAN token into the ASI token.
* The OCEAN:ASI conversion ratio will be 1:0.433226. Therefore, if you currently hold ~2308 OCEAN this would convert to 1000 ASI.
* Scope to increase the ASI token supply by 610,849.199 tokens to accommodate the conversion of the OCEAN tokens.

By voting yes on this proposal you are agreeing to the changes outlined above.

Decentralized governance is important to us. It is also likely that there will be additional governance for future iterations of the merger.
